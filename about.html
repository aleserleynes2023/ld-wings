Two Friends, One Fryer Our Story

L&D Wings started as a weekend stall run by two college friends, Leynes and Dilao, who argued for months over whose wing sauce was better. Instead of picking a winner, they put both on the menu and let customers decide. The line got longer every Saturday.

Today, L&D Wings is a 60-seat restaurant on Maginhawa Street with ten house-made sauces and one simple promise: unli wings, cooked fresh, at a price students can afford.

The blue? It's for the Blue Flame, the hottest part of a fire and the name of our hottest wing. Only a few hundred guests have finished a plate.
